Version 0.2.0 adds two original wallpapers generated with the built-in image-generation tool. They join the existing sandstone-arch wallpaper documented in `artwork.md`. They are bundled assets and do not claim to depict streaming titles or recommendations.

Saved as `app/src/main/assets/gallery/luminous-shore.png`.

Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original 16:9 cinematic wallpaper for a Gallery-style smart TV home screen with a large white clock near the upper center and app dock along bottom. A secluded black volcanic beach at blue hour, luminous bioluminescent turquoise surf tracing a sweeping shoreline from lower left toward the right third, distant dark sea stacks, a few stars in an inky navy sky, very fine natural film grain, realistic large format landscape photography. Wide horizontal frame, serene and wondrous, strong but restrained blue-green light in water, no oversaturated neon. Keep upper center and middle center dark and low-detail for white UI text; brightest glowing wave on right third; quiet dark bottom strip. No people, buildings, text, logos, watermark, border or UI. Output single landscape 1920x1080 or same 16:9 ratio.
