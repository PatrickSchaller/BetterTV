# Cinematic backdrop

`app/src/main/assets/cinema.png` is original artwork generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. It is a wallpaper, not a film recommendation or an image of a real streaming title.

Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original cinematic wallpaper bundled in an offline Android TV launcher. Create a serene, panoramic 16:9 landscape: a massive softly illuminated sandstone arch on the RIGHT THIRD framing distant blue mountains and a small luminous moon at dusk, a shallow reflective desert lake across the foreground, delicate atmospheric haze, editorial large-format film photography with believable natural texture, sumptuous deep indigo and slate-blue shadows and restrained warm amber light on the rock. The LEFT HALF should be very dark, calm negative space, low detail, to support white television UI text. Composition horizontal, horizon at middle, finest landscape detail concentrated in right half, quiet lower quarter where application tiles will appear. Beautiful immersive nature, inviting and sophisticated, no sci-fi objects, no people. No text, no logos, no UI, no borders, no watermarks. Output a single landscape 1920x1080 or similarly proportioned image.

Actual output: 1672 × 941 PNG. The image is bundled and needs no network request. App branding comes from each installed application's own banner/icon.

Design: landscape fills the screen; text sits in the quiet left side; navy shading protects contrast around navigation and app rows. The palette is midnight `#071426`, slate `#17263E`, white `#F5F3EF`, muted blue `#AAB7CA`, and champagne `#EBD4A7`. Native sans-serif text keeps the UI legible from a couch; a warm focus outline identifies the selected control. No autoplay or automatic wallpaper rotation.
